Privacy Policy

Living Battle Maps. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Living Battle Maps does not collect any personal information. I do not run a server for this app, so there is nowhere for your information to go. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

What the app collects

Nothing. In this version there is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third party code that collects data. All of the maps, photographs and text are stored inside the app, and this version does not need an internet connection to show them.

Location

This version does not use your location and does not ask for permission to.

Purchases

This version of the app is free and has no purchases. If a later version asks for a purchase, it will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I do not see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app would only learn from Apple whether the purchase was made, so that it can open the maps.

Data stored on your phone

This version keeps a few settings on your phone, such as which war introductions you have already seen, whether introductions are on, and your sound options. They never leave your phone and are deleted when you delete the app. A later version that asks for a purchase may remember a single yes or no, on your phone only, so that people who installed the free version keep everything. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Links to other websites

A few source links in the battle write-ups open in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and the app does not share anything with them.

Children

This version of the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
