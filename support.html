Common questions

How do I play a battle?
Open a map from the library, then press play. Drag the timeline to move to any hour. Pinch to zoom and drag to pan. In the satellite view you can twist two fingers to rotate the map.
Do I need an internet connection?
Not in this version. Every map, photograph and passage of text is stored in the app.
What does visit mode do with my location?
It shows where you are on the battlefield map. In this version your location stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings under Privacy and Security, then Location Services.
A map is slow on my phone.
The largest maps ask the most of an older phone. Close other apps and try again, and tell me which battle and which phone, because I want to fix it.
I found a mistake in a map or a date.
Please tell me the battle, the time on the clock, and what you think is wrong, with a source if you have one. Where the historical record disagrees with itself, many pages say so, and I would like to hear about sources I have missed.

Where the maps come from

Map data and imagery come from OpenStreetMap contributors (Open Database Licence), the U.S. Geological Survey, Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (modified Copernicus Sentinel data, CC BY 4.0), AWS Terrain Tiles and Natural Earth. Full credits are on the About screen in the app.
